# Intermediate terminal motion reserve

`intermediate_position_v1.py/.json` proves a conditional reserve of **.005m per coordinate** around each terminal-set macro-step position. This covers all four actual RK4 stage inputs, all five substep endpoints, the final macro endpoint, and every linear collision chord whose ends lie in that macro cover. A sphere expansion of **.009m** also suffices, since `3*(.005)^2<(.009)^2` exactly. A continuous physical trajectory is not defined by this numerical simulator; extending this result to a continuous flight curve requires an independent model/interpolation error contract.

The proof uses the full published terminal entry/guard domain, not the eight trim examples. Initial velocity has each component at most .105663m/s, initial rates have components below .297rad/s, and every current/history stored body-rate component is below .5rad/s. Delay interpolation is convex in REAL arithmetic. The interval forward arithmetic trace independently accounts for the Float32 delay/interpolation errors and excludes the actual actuator-clamp branches; its applied body-rate bound remains below .51rad/s.

For the entire physical parameter set, nominal inertia and its represented constants, the rate equation under held u is `w_i'=-lambda_i*w_i+lambda_i*u_i+c_i*w_j*w_k`, with lambda in [27,34], |c_i|<=1. Take W=1rad/s componentwise, |u_i|<=.51, and bound the forcing by `r=34*.51+W²=18.34`. The exact scalar RK4 stage formulas established in `zero_rate_history_v1` give stage bounds 1, .99134, .99269256 and .98538815608, all <=W. The macro endpoint bound is below .928485. The gyro compensation norm is below .018001, so its clip threshold100 remains inactive. Thus every REAL stage has `||w||2<=|wx|+|wy|+|wz|<=3`. No continuous-time rate solution is used.

At a unit REAL quaternion substep start, each RK4 quaternion stage is a finite polynomial in h*w. The geometric bound gives `||qstage||<=N=1/(1-h*3)=500/497`. The original vendor homogeneous rotation obeys `||R(q)e3||2=||q||²` and the original isotropic drag is `d*||q||⁴*v`; these identities do not assume unit unnormalized stages. The positive motor recurrence and battery<=1 preserve the convex hull of initial and target thrust, both below6.5N over the published domain. Thus every stage acceleration component satisfies

`|a_i| <= 6.5/m_min*N² + g_max + d_max/m_min*N⁴*|v_i|`.

Define `A0=6.5/m_min*N²+g_max`, `D=d_max/m_min*N⁴`, H=.01s and `V=(V0+H*A0)/(1-H*D)`. All values and decisions are exact Fractions of the outward published domain endpoints. Here H*D<1, V<.351757986m/s, and the identity `V=V0+H*(A0+D*V)` holds exactly. A causal induction over RK4 nodes proves every stage and endpoint velocity <=V: their positive increment weights bound the cumulative increment by `t*(A0+D*V)` with 0<=t<=H. This is a discrete stage argument, not an ODE comparison. Repeating the same positive-weight argument for position gives `|pstage_i-pmacro_i|<=H*V<.00351758m`.

The Float32 reserve is separate. The helper evaluates the ORIGINAL 13D polynomial dynamics, original optimized quaternion products, original homogeneous rotations, original mass/gravity/thrust conversion, rounded nominal/physical constants and inertia reciprocals, original left-associated RK4 and quaternion normalization. For EACH of the two fractional-delay branches it records all25 stage-input/endpoint position error vectors. Its real intervals plus forward binary32 errors include represented h versus exact1/500, the input raw quaternion8u norm shell versus exact projective q, and verified-decay motor rounding. The largest analytic stage position error is below4.771364e-6m. The deliberately looser1e-4m allowance is therefore checked by an exact Fraction inequality against every node. It is not inferred from a simulation or from the macro-only5e-6 bound.

Combining the two arguments gives a total per-coordinate reserve below **.00361758m**, strictly less than .005m. All covered nodes lie in the same convex box `p_macro+[-.005,.005]^3`, so every straight segment joining such nodes also lies in that box. In particular, each consecutive macro-endpoint collision chord has both ends in its starting macro-step cover. Coverage of arbitrary segments skipping several macro covers is not implied.
